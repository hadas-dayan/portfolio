About Me

Hi, I’m Hadas Dayan, a UI/UX designer who loves to turn ideas into smart, accessible digital experiences.

With a background in digital design and experience working with real users, I focus on creating interfaces that feel natural, fluid, and accurate to the client’s business goals.

I believe that good design starts with a deep understanding of the users, so I research, analyze, and experiment until I arrive at the optimal solution. In my work, I emphasize micro-interactions, visual hierarchy, and aesthetics that combine convenience with beauty.
